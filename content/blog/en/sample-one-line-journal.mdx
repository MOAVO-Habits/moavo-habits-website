## Why just one line

If you plan to write a lot from day one, it is hard to get past day three. Lowering the goal to **one line** makes it much easier to begin.

- Write what you did today in one sentence.
- Add what went well, or what did not.
- Pick it up again tomorrow.

## When entries add up

As the days pile up, your behavior patterns start to show. You can read more on the [app page](/app).

### Start small

A system outlasts willpower. Today, write just one line.
